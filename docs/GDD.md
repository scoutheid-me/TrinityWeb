# Trinity game design

Trinity is an original, single-player, real-time third-person anime fantasy action RPG. Its pillars are **Combat → Discovery → Mastery**. Mechanical skill, experimentation, observation, and discovering secrets should matter more than following objective icons or unlocking every interesting Art through levels.

The current deliverable is one repeatable Combat Lab, the Aether Hall. A Wayfarer faces the Aether Sentinel in a guild training arena. Build SP with measured basics, commit to a multi-hit Art, read enemy telegraphs, dodge or parry, fill Break, and exploit stagger. Exactly four active Art slots support future build choices.

All characters, names, models, effects, and sound cues in this prototype are original. Reference games inform high-level feel only. The target is a modern anime/PBR hybrid, with expressive characters and luminous monumental fantasy architecture. Current models and procedural motion are temporary.

Current scope excludes towns, forests, NPCs, quests, inventory/equipment progression, networking, and a large world. Improve the sword fight before adding breadth. Next milestones: combat feel polish; additional Arts and loadout UI; enemy variety/boss prototype; equipment; trainers and acquisition; Event Ledger and quest systems; then the first region.

Do not present the Combat Lab as the finished RPG. Functional verification and subjective combat polish are separate gates.
